Case Study: Uber NYC Pricing Optimization

1. The Data Strategy

Aggregating millions of Uber trip records from NYC boroughs (Manhattan, Bronx, Queens) using SQL and Google BigQuery to understand spatial demand.

2. Statistical Methods

Applied Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and segmentation to categorize order peaks by day, hour, and pickup location to find inefficiencies.

3. Visualization & Modeling

Developed Tableau dashboards to visualize optimized revenue metrics: Average Distance per Order, Total Passengers, and Pricing vs Response time.

4. Business Impact

Simulated a $688K increase in total optimized revenue per quarter while better distributing driver resources across high-demand areas.
